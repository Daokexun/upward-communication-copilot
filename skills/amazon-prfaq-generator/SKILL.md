# Amazon Working Backwards PR/FAQ Generator

## Structural Standard
Every Working Backwards proposal must contain exactly 3 sections:

### Section 1: The Press Release (1 Page)
1. **Heading**: Product name in a way that target customer understands.
2. **Sub-heading**: One-sentence summary of the product and primary business benefit.
3. **Summary Paragraph**: City, launch date, and summary of the breakthrough.
4. **Problem Paragraph**: The visceral customer problem being solved (quote customer pain).
5. **Solution Paragraph**: How the product effortlessly resolves the problem.
6. **Leader Quote**: Quote from company executive framing strategic alignment.
7. **Customer Quote**: Simulated testimonial highlighting delightful experience.
8. **Call to Action**: Where customers go to use it today.

### Section 2: External FAQs (Customer-Facing)
- Pricing, data privacy, transition from legacy tools, SLA guarantees.

### Section 3: Internal FAQs (Executive-Facing)
- Unit economics & break-even timeline.
- Headcount & infrastructure cost trade-offs.
- Failure scenarios & pre-mortem mitigations.
- Competitor response & defensibility moat.
