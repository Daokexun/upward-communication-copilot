# Executive BLUF & Minto Pyramid SCQA Synthesizer

## Core Architecture
Executives read between meetings in 30-second windows. Eliminate chronological backstory and enforce:

### 1. BLUF (Bottom Line Up Front)
- **Action & Decision Required**: Single sentence stating recommendation, dollar ask, and impact.

### 2. SCQA Narrative Framework
- **S (Situation)**: Established baseline fact everyone agrees on.
- **C (Complication)**: The triggering catalyst or newly surfaced constraint (e.g. latency spike, vendor price hike).
- **Q (Question)**: The exact strategic dilemma requiring leadership input.
- **A (Answer / Recommendation)**: The recommended decision with quantified ROI and compromise analysis.

### 3. Pyramidal Grouping
- Group supporting arguments into MECE buckets (Mutually Exclusive, Collectively Exhaustive):
  - *Financial & Unit Economics*
  - *Engineering Velocity & Technical Debt*
  - *Customer Experience & SLA Risk*
